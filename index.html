POCKET INSPECTOR

Prove your noise problem.

Pocket Inspector is a noise log and decibel meter for iPhone. It measures noise like a sound level meter, catches every disturbance while you sleep, and turns it into a report your landlord, HOA, or council can't ignore.

  • A-weighted dB(A) sound meter
  • Overnight noise logs that keep running with the screen locked
  • Automatic incident capture with short audio clips
  • PDF evidence reports and a ready-to-send complaint letter
  • Plus a level, compass, light meter, metal detector, and altimeter

Private by design: no accounts, no tracking, and nothing leaves your iPhone unless you share it.
