How do I record noise overnight?

On the Home tab, tap Start Noise Log. Leave your iPhone near the noise, plugged in, and lock the screen. Logging continues in the background, and a Live Activity shows the current level on your Lock Screen. Tap Stop & Save Log in the morning.

What counts as an incident?

Any sound that stays above your incident level (55 dB(A) by default) for at least half a second. Set the level in Settings → Noise Log. Each incident records its time, duration, peak, and average, plus a short audio clip that starts a few seconds before the noise began.

How accurate is it?

Readings are A-weighted estimates from the iPhone microphone, typically close to a consumer sound meter. To match a reference meter, adjust Settings → Sound Meter → Calibration Offset. Pocket Inspector is not a certified measurement instrument.

How do I share evidence?

Open a saved log in the Evidence tab and tap Report. You can export a PDF report with a minute-by-minute chart and incident log, or a pre-filled complaint letter to edit and send.

Managing your subscription

Pocket Inspector Pro is billed by Apple. To cancel or change plans, open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions, or manage subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, tap Restore on the Pro screen.
