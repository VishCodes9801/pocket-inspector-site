POCKET INSPECTOR

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

The short version: Pocket Inspector does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything the app measures or records stays on your iPhone.

What the app accesses, and why

Pocket Inspector asks for each permission only when you open the tool that needs it. All processing happens on your device.

  • Microphone: to measure sound levels. During a noise log, if incident clips are turned on, short audio clips of loud moments are saved on your iPhone. You can turn clips off in Settings → Noise Log. Clips are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Camera: to estimate light levels from the camera's exposure settings. No photos or video frames are captured or stored.
  • Location (while using the app): so the compass can point to true north. Your location is not stored or shared.
  • Motion sensors: for the level, metal detector, altimeter, and motion dashboard.

Data stored on your device

Saved readings, noise logs, incident clips, and notes are stored locally on your iPhone (and in a shared container so the app's widgets can show your latest reading). They are included in your device backups according to your iPhone's backup settings. Deleting a saved record deletes its clips. Deleting the app deletes everything.

Sharing

Data leaves your iPhone only when you choose to share it, for example by exporting a PDF report or a complaint letter through the share sheet. Where it goes then is up to you.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Pocket Inspector receives no payment details. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Recording responsibly

Laws on recording audio vary by location. Incident clips are meant to document noise disturbances. Please don't use them to record private conversations without the consent the law requires where you are.

Children

Pocket Inspector does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
